1. Your task
Create a C++ program that checks whether a student qualifies for a scholarship.
Your program should:
1. Declare int marks = 85 and int attendance = 90.
2. Check whether marks are greater than or equal to 80.
3. Check whether attendance is greater than or equal to 75.
4. Use the logical AND operator (&&) to determine whether the student qualifies.
5. Print all three results.
2. Expected output
Marks requirement met: 1
Attendance requirement met: 1
Eligible for scholarship: 1
