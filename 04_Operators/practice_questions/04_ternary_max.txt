1. Your task
Write a C++ program that finds the largest of three integers using the ternary operator (?:).
Your program should:
1. Declare three integers: a = 25, b = 40, and c = 30.
2. Use a ternary expression to find the largest number.
3. Print all three numbers and the largest value.
2. Expected output
First number: 25
Second number: 40
Third number: 30
Largest number: 40
