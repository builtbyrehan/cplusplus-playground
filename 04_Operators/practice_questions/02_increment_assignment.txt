1. Your task
Write a C++ program that:
1. Declares int x = 10.
2. Prints the value of x before any changes.
3. Uses x += 5 and prints the updated value.
4. Uses x *= 2 and prints the updated value.
5. Prints x++, then prints x again.
6. Uses --x and prints the result.
2. Expected output
Initial value: 10
After += 5: 15
After *= 2: 30
Post-increment: 30
After post-increment: 31
Pre-decrement: 30
